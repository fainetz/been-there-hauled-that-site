How to Get Rid of a Mattress in Eagan and Dakota County (2026)

By Been There, Hauled That · November 18, 2026

Short answer: Dakota County's Recycling Zone in Eagan does not take mattresses or box springs, so in the South Metro your options are a paid drop-off (Hennepin County's Brooklyn Park site charges $25 per piece), a city cleanup day (Shakopee charges $30 per piece), donating a clean one to Bridging, extra-fee bulky pickup through your trash hauler, or a junk removal pickup, which typically runs about $55 to $150 for one mattress.

Can I donate my mattress?

Only if it's clean. Bridging accepts twin through king mattresses with no stains or rips. Habitat ReStore and Goodwill do not take mattresses or box springs. Cribs, air mattresses and sleeper sofas are refused at Bridging too.

Can I put a mattress out with the trash?

Not with regular weekly trash. Eagan, Burnsville, Apple Valley and Lakeville don't run city garbage service; you contract with a licensed private hauler, and bulky items like mattresses are an extra-fee pickup you schedule with them. Ask your hauler for their price before you set it out.

Is there a mattress law in Minnesota?

Not yet. As of 2026 Minnesota has no mattress ban or recycling fee in force. A bill for a $5 mattress fee was heard in committee in March 2026 and did not pass.

Questions people ask

Does the Recycling Zone in Eagan take mattresses?

No. Dakota County's Recycling Zone does not accept mattresses or box springs.

How much does it cost to get rid of a mattress in the Twin Cities?

Drop-off fees run about $25 to $30 per piece (Hennepin County, Shakopee Clean Up Day). Junk removal pickup typically costs about $55 to $150.

Can I donate a used mattress in the Twin Cities?

Yes, if it has no stains or rips. Bridging accepts twin through king. Habitat ReStore and Goodwill do not take mattresses.

Is a box spring charged separately?

Usually. Drop-off sites charge per piece, and Hennepin County counts a split box spring as two pieces.
