How to Get Rid of an Old Couch in Eagan, Burnsville or Apple Valley

By Been There, Hauled That · November 17, 2026

Short answer: You have four main options: donate it if it's in good shape, ask your garbage hauler about bulky-item pickup, list it free online, or book a junk removal company to carry it out. Note that Dakota County's Recycling Zone in Eagan does not accept furniture or mattresses.

1. Donate it (if it's in good shape)

Clean couches without rips, stains or pet damage can often be donated. Call ahead, since many donation centers are picky about upholstered furniture.

2. Ask your garbage hauler

Many South Metro cities use private garbage haulers, and many haulers offer bulky-item pickup for a fee. Check your hauler's website or call them.

3. Give it away online

Listing it free on Facebook Marketplace or a neighborhood group can work, but you'll need to coordinate pickup and may get no-shows.

4. Book junk removal

We'll carry it out from anywhere in the house, so you don't have to move it to the curb. If it's donatable, we'll donate it.

Where not to take it

Dakota County's Recycling Zone in Eagan handles household hazardous waste and electronics, but does not take furniture or mattresses.

FAQ

Does the Recycling Zone in Eagan take couches?

No. Dakota County's Recycling Zone does not accept furniture or mattresses.

Will a junk removal company take a couch from my basement?

Yes. We carry items out from basements, upstairs rooms and garages.
