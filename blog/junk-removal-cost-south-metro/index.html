How Much Does Junk Removal Cost in Eagan and the South Metro?

By Been There, Hauled That · November 17, 2026

Short answer: Most junk removal jobs in the U.S. cost about $150 to $350, with national averages around $225 to $250. A single item like a mattress often runs $60 to $150, and a full truckload commonly runs $600 to $800. Your exact price depends on how much you have and what it is, so the fastest way to know is to send a photo for an up-front quote.

Typical junk removal prices

Published 2026 cost guides land in similar ranges. These are national figures; South Metro quotes vary with the job:

  • Single item (mattress, recliner, small appliance): about $60 to $150
  • Large appliance (fridge, washer): about $50 to $150 per item
  • Small or partial load: about $75 to $500 depending on size
  • Full truckload: about $600 to $800

Sources: LawnStarter, HireAHelper, 1-800-GOT-JUNK.

What changes the price?

  • Volume: how much of the van it fills is the biggest factor.
  • Weight and type: heavy debris and appliances cost more to dispose of.
  • Access: basements, attics and stairs take more time.
  • Disposal fees: some items carry landfill or recycling fees.
