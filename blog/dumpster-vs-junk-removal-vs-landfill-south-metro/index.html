Dumpster vs Junk Removal vs Hauling It Yourself: What's Cheapest in the South Metro?

By Been There, Hauled That · November 18, 2026

Short answer: For a few bulky items or a single room, junk removal is usually cheapest once you count your time, because the price includes the labor and the disposal fees. Hauling it yourself only wins if you have a truck and the items are accepted at a drop-off you can reach. A dumpster makes sense for multi-day remodels where you'll fill it slowly over a week.

Hauling it yourself: the catch

  • The Recycling Zone (3365 Dodd Rd, Eagan; Wed 9–8, Thu 12–8, Fri 9–5, Sat 8–5; ID required, open to residents of the 7 metro counties) doesn't take furniture, mattresses or large appliances.
  • Landfills like Burnsville Landfill (2650 Cliff Rd W) and Pine Bend charge by weight, often with a minimum charge; Pine Bend's minimum is 1 ton. Call ahead for fees.
  • Mattresses and appliances go to separate places with separate fees.

Dumpsters: the catch

You still do all the lifting, there are weight limits and banned items (appliances, mattresses, electronics), and your driveway is blocked for days. Some cities require a permit for street placement.

Junk removal: the catch

Price depends on how much space your items take in the truck, so get a quote first. With us, a photo gets you an up-front price, we carry everything out, donate what's usable, and 10–20% goes to a local homeless shelter or dog rescue.

Questions people ask

Is it cheaper to rent a dumpster or hire junk removal?

For a few items or one room, junk removal is usually cheaper once labor and disposal fees are included. Dumpsters suit multi-day remodels.

Can I take junk to the Burnsville landfill myself?

Yes, Burnsville Landfill at 2650 Cliff Rd W accepts self-haul loads. Call for current fees and accepted items.

What can't go in a dumpster?

Typically appliances, mattresses, electronics, tires and hazardous waste, which need separate disposal.
