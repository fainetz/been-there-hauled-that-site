Hot Tub Removal in the South Metro: Cost and What to Do Before Pickup

By Been There, Hauled That · November 18, 2026

Short answer: Hot tub removal typically costs about $200 to $600, depending on size, access and whether it has to be cut apart. Before pickup, turn off the power at the breaker and have an electrician disconnect the hard-wired circuit, then drain the water. Most tubs are cut into pieces so they fit through gates and into a truck.

What changes the price

  • Size: a 2-person tub vs. an 8-person swim spa.
  • Access: a clear path from the patio to the driveway vs. a fenced backyard, stairs or a deck.
  • Whole or cut up: cutting the shell takes time but avoids damage to fences and lawns.
  • Extras: steps, cover, cover lifter and any surrounding decking.
WhatTypical price
Hot tub removal (typical)about $200–$600

Before we arrive

  1. Kill the power at the breaker. Hot tubs are usually hard-wired; have an electrician disconnect the circuit.
  2. Drain it a day ahead with the drain spigot or a sump pump.
  3. Clear a path and unlock gates.

Where does it go?

Metal, motors and pumps are recycled where possible; the shell and frame usually go to a landfill. A working tub in good shape can sometimes be resold.
