What Junk Removal Can't Take (and Where It Goes in Dakota County)

By Been There, Hauled That · November 17, 2026

Short answer: Junk removal companies generally can't take hazardous materials like paint, solvents, pesticides, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos or medical waste. Dakota County residents can bring many household hazardous wastes to The Recycling Zone at 3365 Dodd Road in Eagan, where most items are free for residents.

Items we can't haul

  • Paint, stains and solvents
  • Pesticides and pool chemicals
  • Gasoline, motor oil and propane tanks
  • Asbestos and medical waste

Where they go: The Recycling Zone in Eagan

Dakota County's Recycling Zone (3365 Dodd Road, Eagan) accepts household hazardous waste from residents, and most items are free. It also takes items like batteries and electronics (some electronics have a fee). It does not take furniture, mattresses, appliances, carpet, lumber or yard waste. Check the county's page or call before you go, since lists and hours change.

What we can take

Furniture, mattresses, appliances, garage and basement clutter, yard debris and light construction debris. We serve Eagan, Burnsville, Apple Valley, Lakeville, Farmington, Shakopee and Edina.

FAQ

Where can I drop off old paint in Dakota County?

Residents can bring household hazardous waste like paint to The Recycling Zone at 3365 Dodd Road in Eagan.

Does junk removal take paint cans?

Not liquid paint or chemicals. Those should go to a household hazardous waste site like The Recycling Zone.
