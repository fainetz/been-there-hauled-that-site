Does My City Have Bulk Trash Pickup? Eagan, Burnsville, Apple Valley and Lakeville

By Been There, Hauled That · November 18, 2026

Short answer: Eagan, Burnsville, Apple Valley and Lakeville do not run city garbage or bulk pickup. Each licenses private haulers, so large items go through your own hauler for an extra fee, a Dakota Valley Recycling bulky-item event, a drop-off site, or a junk removal company. Shakopee is different: it has one city hauler, Dick's Sanitation, which does bulk pickup for an added fee, plus an annual Clean Up Day.

What is Dakota Valley Recycling?

Dakota Valley Recycling (DVR) is the shared recycling staff for Apple Valley, Burnsville, Eagan and Lakeville. It runs bulky-item collection events during the year, some free and some with small fees. Check its site for the next date.

Drop-off options

  • The Recycling Zone (3365 Dodd Rd, Eagan; Wed 9–8, Thu 12–8, Fri 9–5, Sat 8–5; ID required, open to residents of the 7 metro counties): hazardous waste, electronics ($10 per TV), small appliances, scrap metal. Not furniture, mattresses or large appliances.
  • Hennepin County drop-offs in Bloomington and Brooklyn Park: large appliances $15, TVs $10; mattresses $25 at Brooklyn Park only. No furniture.
  • Burnsville Landfill (2650 Cliff Rd W) and transfer stations: call for fees; landfills usually charge by weight with a minimum.

When a junk removal pickup makes more sense

If you have more than one or two items, don't have a truck, or it's in a basement, one pickup is usually simpler than a hauler fee plus trips to several drop-offs. We take furniture, mattresses and appliances in a single visit across Eagan, Burnsville, Apple Valley, Lakeville, Farmington, Shakopee and Edina, and donate what we can.

Questions people ask

Does Eagan have bulk trash pickup?

No. Eagan doesn't provide garbage service; residents schedule bulky pickup with their licensed private hauler or use a junk removal company.

Does Burnsville pick up large items?

No. Burnsville licenses private haulers and does not handle pickup; ask your hauler about bulky-item fees.

Does Apple Valley have a cleanup day?

Apple Valley, Burnsville, Eagan and Lakeville share Dakota Valley Recycling, which runs bulky-item collection events during the year.

Where can I drop off furniture in Dakota County?

The Recycling Zone doesn't take furniture. Donate usable pieces to Bridging or Habitat ReStore, or use a landfill, transfer station or junk removal pickup.
