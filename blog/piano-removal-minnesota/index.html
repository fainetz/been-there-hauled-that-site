How to Get Rid of an Old Piano in Minnesota

By Been There, Hauled That · November 18, 2026

Short answer: Most old upright pianos can't be donated, because tuning, moving and repairs cost more than they're worth. Try a free listing first, and if no one takes it, a junk removal or piano mover will haul it; typical prices run about $200 to $550, with a national average around $431.

Step 1: try to rehome it

List it free online with clear photos and the brand. Schools, churches and community centers occasionally take a piano that's in good shape. Be honest about tuning and condition, and give the buyer a deadline.

Step 2: if no one wants it

WhatTypical price
Piano removal (typical range)about $150–$550
National averageabout $431

The price depends on stairs, distance to the truck and whether it's an upright, a spinet or a grand.

Why is piano removal expensive?

Uprights often weigh 300 to 800 pounds and are top-heavy. Moving one safely takes several people and the right equipment, especially from a basement.

What happens to it

The metal plate, strings and hardware can be recycled; wood and the rest are disposed of properly. Usable benches and stools are donated.
