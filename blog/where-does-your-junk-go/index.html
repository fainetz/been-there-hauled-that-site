Where Does Your Junk Go After a Junk Removal Pickup?

By Been There, Hauled That · November 17, 2026

Short answer: After a pickup, usable items get donated, old towels and blankets go to dog shelters, and what's left is recycled or disposed of properly. On top of that, 10–20% of every job's payment goes to one local homeless shelter or dog rescue, and you get a photo and video of who it helped.

Step 1: Donate what's usable

Furniture and household goods in good shape go to donation centers instead of the landfill.

Step 2: Towels and blankets to dog shelters

Shelters always need old towels and blankets for bedding and bath time, so we set them aside.

Step 3: Recycle or dispose of the rest properly

Whatever can't be reused is recycled where possible and disposed of properly.

Step 4: Your job helps a person or a dog

10–20% of each job's payment goes to one South Metro organization: a homeless shelter or a dog rescue. We text you a photo and video of who your job helped, and it's yours to share. Photos and videos of people are only taken and shared with their permission.
