Garage Cleanout Checklist for Minnesota Homeowners (Before Winter)

By Been There, Hauled That · November 17, 2026

Short answer: Clear your garage before the snow: pull everything into keep, donate and toss piles; set aside paint and chemicals for a hazardous waste drop-off; make room for the car, snow blower and shovels; then have the toss pile hauled away in one trip.

The checklist

  1. Pick a dry day and pull everything out to the driveway.
  2. Sort into three piles: keep, donate, toss.
  3. Set aside hazardous items like paint, oil and chemicals for a hazardous waste drop-off.
  4. Plan winter space: car, snow blower, shovels, salt and boots near the door.
  5. Go vertical: shelves and wall hooks free up floor space.
  6. Haul the toss pile in one trip so it doesn't creep back in.

FAQ

When is the best time to clean out a garage in Minnesota?

Fall, before snow, so the car and snow equipment fit for winter.
