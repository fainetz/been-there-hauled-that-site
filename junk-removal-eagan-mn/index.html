Does Eagan pick up large items?

Eagan does not provide city garbage service. Residents pick a licensed private hauler, and large items are an extra-fee pickup you schedule with that hauler. Dakota Valley Recycling, which Eagan shares with Apple Valley, Burnsville and Lakeville, runs bulky-item events during the year. (source)

Where things go near Eagan

The Recycling Zone is right here in Eagan at 3365 Dodd Rd (Hwy 149). It takes paint, batteries, electronics ($10 per TV), small appliances and scrap metal, but not furniture, mattresses or large appliances.
