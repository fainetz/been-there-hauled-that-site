About · Eagan & South Metro

About Been There, Hauled That

Been There, Hauled That is a local junk removal business based in Eagan, Minnesota, serving Eagan, Burnsville, Apple Valley, Lakeville, Farmington, Shakopee and Edina. We haul furniture, appliances and cleanouts, donate what's usable, and send 10–20% of every job to a local homeless shelter or a dog rescue.

Why we started

Most junk removal ends at the landfill. We wanted every haul to do some good for the South Metro: a share of every job goes to people experiencing homelessness or to dogs waiting for homes, and you get a photo and video of who your job helped.

What we haul

Furniture, mattresses, appliances, garage and basement cleanouts, estate and move-out cleanouts, yard debris and light construction debris. We carry it out from anywhere in the house.

How it works

  1. Text a photo of what's going.
  2. Get a price up front.
  3. We load it and haul it, often the same day.
  4. You get a photo and video of who your job helped.
