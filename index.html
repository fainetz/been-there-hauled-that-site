FAQ

Good questions.

Where do you work?

Richfield and the surrounding Twin Cities area. If you're not sure we cover you, just ask.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how much there is and what it is. Send a photo and we'll give you a price before we start. No surprises.

How do you pay?

Cash or a payment app. Payment is due when the job is done.

Do I need to move things outside first?

No. We'll carry items out from wherever they are: basement, attic, garage or upstairs.

What can't you take?

Hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, fuel and asbestos. Ask us about anything you're unsure of.
