FAQ

Good questions.

What areas do you serve?

We do junk removal across the South Metro: Eagan, Burnsville, Apple Valley, Lakeville, Farmington, Shakopee and Edina, plus nearby cities. If you're not sure we cover you, just ask.

How much does junk removal cost in the South Metro?

It depends on how much there is and what it is. Send us a photo and we'll give you a price before we start. See our junk removal cost guide for typical ranges.

Who does my job help?

Each job sends 10–20% of its payment to one local organization: a homeless shelter or a dog rescue. Afterward we text you a photo and video of who it helped, yours to share.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how much there is and what it is. Text a photo to (612) 735-5424 and we'll give you a price before we start. No surprises.

Do you offer same-day junk removal?

Yes, when our schedule allows. Message us a photo early in the day and we'll tell you if we can get it out today. See same-day junk removal in the South Metro.

How do you pay?

Cash or a payment app. Payment is due when the job is done.

Do I need to move things outside first?

No. We'll carry items out from wherever they are: basement, attic, garage or upstairs.

What can't you take?

Hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, fuel and asbestos. Ask us about anything you're unsure of.
